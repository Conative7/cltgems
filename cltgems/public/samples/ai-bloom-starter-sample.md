## 3. Example AI follow-up scripts (kit)

Copy-paste and swap the brackets. Tone: plain, respectful, short.

### A. Quote follow-up (text or email)
Hi [Name] — just checking you got the quote for [job / address] ([price]). Happy to adjust timing if [other day] works better. — [Your name], [Business]

### B. Invoice reminder (friendly)
Hi [Name] — friendly reminder that invoice #[number] for $[amount] is due [date]. Zelle/check details are on the invoice. Thank you! — [Your name]

### C. Past-due polite nudge
Hi [Name] — invoice #[number] ($[amount]) is a few days past due. If something’s off or you need a payment plan, just say the word — we’re easy to work with. — [Your name], [Business]

### D. Thank-you after payment
Thanks [Name]! Payment received for #[number]. Glad we could help with [job]. If you need us again (or know a neighbor), we’re here. — [Your name]

### E. LinkedIn “just completed a job” post
Wrapped another [type of job] in [neighborhood / Charlotte]. Clean process, clear invoice, paid on time — that’s the goal every week. If your [home / job site] needs the same, send a note. #Charlotte #SmallBusiness #[Trade]

### F. Soft rebook (optional)
Hi [Name] — it’s been about [X weeks] since we [cleaned / finished]. Want the same day/time next month? I can hold [two options]. — [Your name]

### G. After “I’ll think about it”
Totally fine, [Name]. Quote for [job] stays good through [date]. If priorities change, reply here and we’ll pick a slot. — [Your name]
